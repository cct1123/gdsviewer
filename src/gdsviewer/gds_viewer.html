GDS Viewer

Inspect GDS layouts, toggle layers and cells, and measure geometry.

Load GDS

Use the picker or drag a .gds file onto the viewer.

Measure: press m, left click two points, hold Ctrl to lock horizontal or vertical, right drag to pan. Zoom: hold z to zoom in and hold x to zoom out at the mouse location.

Cells

Click a cell to show or hide that branch.
